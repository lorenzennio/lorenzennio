### Hi, I'm Lorenz 👋

I currently work on **data analytics methods and statistics in particle physics**. My research focusses on [accelerating science][arxiv], bringing us new breakthroughs faster.

I hold a PhD in Physics from the LMU in Munich, I have worked in the German parliament as scientific advisor, and at the [International Atomic Energy Agency][iaea].

In my free time, I get excited about brainstorming how to deal with the impact of AI, fighting climate change, travelling, climbing and mountaineering.

More about me and my work: [lorenzgaertner.com][website]
