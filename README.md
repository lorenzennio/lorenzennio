### Hi, I'm Lorenz 👋

I work on **data analytics methods and statistics in particle physics** — my research focuses on [accelerating science][arxiv], bringing new breakthroughs faster.

I hold a PhD in Physics from LMU Munich, and have worked as a scientific advisor in the German parliament and at the [International Atomic Energy Agency][iaea].

Outside of physics, I get excited about the impact of AI, fighting climate change, travelling, climbing and mountaineering.

More about me and my work: [lorenzennio.github.io][website]
